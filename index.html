HRDPS Wind and SSCOFS Current Maps

About this map · what’s new

One map, Queen Charlotte Sound → Puget Sound. Navigate to where you paddle, then flip layer at the same view coordinates: SSCOFS currents (native FVCOM mesh) → HRDPS 2.5 km wind. build 2026-08-09a (the Wave Height display is easier to read at the rough end: above about 1.5 m its colours now run indigo → magenta instead of three near-identical greys, so a gale separates from a stiff blow at a glance. The trade, taken deliberately, is that calm water under 0.5 m separates a little less than before — a faint wash on benign water is the intended reading. Its key is now a true 0–3 m scale with evenly spaced marks, so the colours bunch to the left where the heights actually are. The Key panel's explanatory prose is collapsed behind a click-to-open line — everything that decodes a mark (the colour bar, the arrow swatches, the Beaufort strip) stays visible, and the map now starts higher up the page. The readout waits 0.3 s before it opens on hover, and once open refreshes at most ten times a second rather than tracking every mouse movement; 2026-08-08c: the Waves layer is public again after its 2026-08-04 withdrawal — the under-read that caused it has been corrected and re-checked against WaveWatch III. Three displays under Sea State: Wave Steepness — rings from Medium up, so water with no ring is unrated, not calm; Wave Height (new) — the wind-sea height in metres, continuous, and wind sea only, no ocean swell; Quartering (beta) — the worst heading to be steering, direction only and uncalibrated. Still a wind-against-current heuristic, not a wave forecast; 2026-08-08a: the wave model was refitted against WaveWatch III. It read heights about 1.5× too high and now reads within about 5%, so good paddling windows it wrongly condemned drop from about 42% to 22%; 2026-08-07a: base legibility under the wind fill — one base map now, USGS topo, in every layer. Its water is darkened by a colour transform applied to the base tiles themselves, so land and water separate by tone and the coastline reads as the boundary between them even under a 70% fill; a light contrast + saturation lift keeps terrain and place names readable. The base-map selector and the alternative bases have been removed. This description is now collapsed by default — open it with About this map above. The desktop gains the ➤ On/Off button beside Fill opacity that hides the arrows without losing the arrow density you were on (the phone has had it since 2026-07-22i). In the Roughness layer the display selector is now Sea State and its displays are Wave Steepness (was "Rings on wind") and Quartering (beta) (was "On your course"); the Shaded cells display has been removed. The Low call is gone from that layer: rings now start at Medium, and Medium draws grey rather than amber, so only High (orange) and Very high (magenta) read as warnings — water with no ring is unrated, not calm. The optional wind arrows over that display are now black on a white casing (they were white on a dark one). The bands themselves are unchanged: this is what the layer reports, not how it is computed; 2026-08-04a: the wind-against-current Roughness layer was temporarily withdrawn (restored 2026-08-08c): compared band for band against WaveWatch III, a peer-reviewed wave model, it read a band low in the seas that matter most — where WW3 called the water "High" the roughness layer painted "Medium" about three quarters of the time, and where WW3 called it "Low" it painted "Medium". It stayed off until that was corrected and re-verified against WW3; the wind and current layers were unaffected and unchanged; 2026-07-23a: Currents: zoomed in past z10, the whole-coast overview now draws alongside the native mesh instead of being switched off by it — a mesh region whose bbox merely clips the corner of your view no longer blanks the modelled water everywhere else on screen (WCVI, Clayoquot to Barkley, was losing a full screen of currents to an 800 m pan), and the tap readout comes back with it; 2026-07-22l: Wind Key legend condensed — Beaufort force number + wind range only, descriptor dropped to shrink the Key panel; 2026-07-22k: Roughness: tapping exposed water outside the analyzed zones now explains why it isn't scored there — the wind-vs-current estimate doesn't model ocean swell — instead of showing a blank readout that looked "safe"; build 2026-07-22j (Course (steer) select in Roughness → the quartering display now shows a compass arrow beside it, pointing the selected heading, phone + desktop; build 2026-07-22i (phone: Wind arrows checkbox (Roughness/Rings mode) now hidden — redundant with the ➤ On/Off arrows button, desktop keeps it; phone: ➤ On/Off button beside Fill opacity toggles the arrows; Fill opacity now runs 0–100% (0 = fill off, arrows/mesh only); phone: Region selector removed (navigate by pinch-and-pan), double-tap-to-zoom disarmed on the controls; Key panel opens full-width at the bottom of the page, ± buttons flank the Fill opacity slider; uniform arrow lattice at every zoom; timeline ◀ ▶ hour-step buttons replace play, bigger map — Base map fixed, densest arrows, controls trimmed; offline: save base + region packs for airplane-mode use, two-clock staleness banner; shared physics.js)
