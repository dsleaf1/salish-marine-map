2 · The control — is the transform really the cause?

P · no transforms at allwhat L_DISABLE_3D gives want BLACK
If P is black while M/N/O are blue, the tile transform is confirmed as the cause and L_DISABLE_3D is the only CSS-level route left — at the cost of Leaflet’s smooth pan/zoom, globally, for every layer.

If P and Q are both blue, the transform is innocent: Safari simply won’t run a url() filter on an <img>, every CSS route dies together, and the canvas in section 4 is the answer.
